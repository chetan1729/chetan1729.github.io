Research

Preprint · July 2026 · Chetan Prakash

Computational or Data-Specific? Distinguishing Sparse Autoencoder Features via Distribution Shift

I study whether sparse autoencoder features reflect a model’s computation or regularities in its training data. This work uses distribution shift to test whether feature directions remain recoverable and their causal effects stay consistent. In a GPT-2-scale model, recoverability predicted causal consistency after controlling for feature frequency and layer.

Selected projects

January 2026 · Language model pretraining

optiGPT

A 124M-parameter decoder-only Transformer built and trained from scratch on FineWeb-EDU. The training pipeline combines distributed training, Flash Attention, and mixed precision across four RTX 3090 GPUs. Achieved 30.27% zero-shot HellaSwag accuracy.

February 2026 · Preference optimization

Directrix

Aligning Gemma-2B with Direct Preference Optimization on Anthropic’s HH-RLHF dataset. Developed a custom merge protocol for LoRA adapters in grouped-query attention kernels and evaluated the tradeoffs between alignment and STEM performance using JAX. Achieved 62.2% preference accuracy.

May 2026 · Vision Transformer training

Marr

Training an 86M-parameter ViT-Base from scratch on ImageNet-1K with distributed training, data augmentation, and regularization. Built a streaming data pipeline and adapted positional embeddings for higher-resolution training, reaching 67.01% top-1 accuracy.
